Stuck on You came out 3 days ago. This is release week; the numbers below are the first read. The artist sits at 9.8M monthly listeners and 64k Spotify followers.

Of the singles, What If I Call is the one working: 44.8M plays in 73 days, about 614k a day on average, Spotify popularity 84. That is a soft lead-in to the release and worth naming out loud rather than hoping it turns.

Catalog is doing real work: What If I Call (Merry Christmas, i miss you) still averages 122k plays a day 676 days after release, and the tracked catalog holds 85.7M plays. A new release lifts catalog if the catalog is easy to find from it.

Growing: i'll wait for you.
