This week's read written from the numbers below, 2026-10-09

Proceed With Caution came out 7 days ago. This is release week; the numbers below are the first read. The artist sits at 836k monthly listeners and 336k Spotify followers.

Of the singles, I Kinda Like That is the one working: 3.9M plays in 78 days, about 50k a day on average, Spotify popularity 66. The newest single, That’s Not Fair, is at 345k plays after 35 days, roughly 10k a day, which is 20% of I Kinda Like That's pace. That is a soft lead-in to the release and worth naming out loud rather than hoping it turns.

Of the brand-new tracks, Living Hell is the one listeners are picking: about 31k plays a day, popularity 55 after 7 days. That already outpaces Good For Me, That’s Not Fair from the single campaign. The rest: Stoner Mentality (13k/day), It’s Over (11k/day).

Catalog is doing real work: Do Me Like That still averages 42k plays a day 385 days after release, and the tracked catalog holds 34.9M plays. A new release lifts catalog if the catalog is easy to find from it.

Growing: Good For Me.

Audience versus routing: the top listener cities are Chicago (20k), Los Angeles (14k), New York City (14k), London (14k), Philadelphia (13k). Chicago is the number one city and has no tour date. That is either a routing gap or a second-leg market, and either way it is a geo-targeting priority.

Short-form: the last three weeks of TikTok posts are landing around 8k views each, against pinned posts at 10.3M. The account has 1.0M followers, so reach is there when the hook is. Release week needs a hook-led post plan, not announcement posts.

Release ops: 6 checklist items are inside their window and not visible from outside the label: Spotify editorial pitch (focus track); Apple Music / Amazon pitches; Canvas + lyrics synced (Musixmatch); Release-week content calendar locked. Worth a two-line confirm with whoever owns them.

Do this week

  1. Living Hell is the organic pick off the EP. Get it into the focus-track slot on the artist profile and the DSP follow-up pitches this week, and test it as the next radio or video candidate before the tour starts.
  2. Make I Kinda Like That the anchor in every DSP pitch and ad creative for release week, not That’s Not Fair. The data says which one people are choosing.
  3. Put Do Me Like That and the next catalog track on Discovery Mode for release week, and make sure the artist profile pins the new EP above them.
  4. Run tour-market geo ads in the routed cities from two weeks out, and treat Chicago, London as the first markets to test for a second leg.
